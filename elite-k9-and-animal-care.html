01 · The Handler

Trained by someone who thinks like a dog first.

Elite K9 and Animal Care is built on a lifelong love for dogs and a deep, practical understanding of how they think. Years of hands-on experience in obedience, general training, and aggression rehabilitation sit behind every session — the kind of instinct that only comes from genuinely connecting with the animal, not just correcting it.

Every dog that comes through — whether a nervous rescue, a boisterous puppy, or a working breed like a Caucasian Shepherd — is trained as an individual. The goal isn't a dog that performs on command. It's a dog that's genuinely easier, calmer, and safer to live with.
